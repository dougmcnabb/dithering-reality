What to Try in the Simulator

  • Toggle 1-Knots Only vs. Residual (<0.5): Past the percolation radius, discrete 1-knots shut off completely (forming a compact visible galaxy), while the un-knotted residual smoothly carries the outer 1/R² wave and keeps v_total(r) flat.
  • Check Inner-Core Dark Fraction: In the dense center, voxels snap up to 1 as often as down to 0, so positive and negative quantization errors cancel near 0% (naturally avoiding an inner dark cusp).
  • Drag Diffusion α down to 0.00: Naive rounding without error diffusion collapses outer structure. Bring α back to 0.75 (Bill Atkinson's 1984 6/8 dither constant) and click New Random Seed: every individual 1-knot moves, yet the global rotation curve is invariant.
  • Verify in Python: Run error_diffusion_galaxy.py (~65 lines, NumPy only) to reproduce the exact 3D voxel numbers locally.
